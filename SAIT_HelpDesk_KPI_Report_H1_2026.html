Strategic Recommendations
🎯

Implement SLA Tiering by Priority

Establish formal SLA targets: High priority (★★★) tickets should be resolved within 24–48 hours; standard tickets within 5 business days. Currently 57 high-priority tickets remain pending with no defined deadline. Enforce escalation alerts at 50% and 100% of SLA window.

High Priority
⚖️

Rebalance Engineer Workload

Top 4 engineers (Asslam, Anoop, Nahid, Zakiyya) handle 73% of all tickets. This concentration creates burnout risk and bottlenecks. Redistribute pending tickets to mid-load engineers (BOVY, Muhammad Faisal, PARAMESHWAR) who collectively have handled only 71 tickets versus the top 4's 362.

High Priority
📋

Launch a Pending Ticket Sprint

Organize a focused 2-week closure sprint targeting the 156 open tickets, starting with the 5 critical clients (MOJ, MoCCAE, EAD, ADQCC, SSA) which collectively hold 97 pending tickets. Assign dedicated engineers per client for this sprint and track daily closures on a visible dashboard.

High Priority
🔁

Build a Recurring Issue Knowledge Base

Multiple clients (especially ADQCC) log the same issues repeatedly — attendance sync errors, leave not reflecting, missing punches. Creating a self-service knowledge base and FAQ for common issues could deflect 20–30% of repeat tickets, reducing volume and freeing engineer capacity.

Medium Priority
📅

Introduce Weekly Pending Ticket Reviews

Mandate weekly reviews of all tickets open beyond 7 days. The current data shows tickets aging well beyond acceptable resolution windows with no documented update (e.g., HD/SAT/05861/26 — 111 days pending; HD/SAT/05840/26 — 118 days pending). Ageing reports should be shared with team leads every Monday.

Medium Priority
📊

Set Monthly Resolution Rate Targets

The resolution rate has fallen from a healthy 93% in January to just 42% in May and 12% in June. Establish a minimum monthly target of 80% resolution rate. Introduce a real-time KPI dashboard visible to all engineers and management, with automated alerts when the rate drops below 70% mid-month.

Medium Priority
🤝

Assign Dedicated Account Engineers

High-volume clients (MOJ, MoCCAE, Zayed Higher Organisation) generate 47% of all tickets yet lack consistent engineer ownership. Assigning a primary and backup engineer per top-5 client improves familiarity, reduces ramp-up time per ticket, and increases client satisfaction.

Longer Term
📱

Explore Remote / Telephonic First-Response

Support Calls (55 tickets, 76% closed) outperform Service visits on resolution rate. Instituting a mandatory remote diagnosis step before field dispatch could convert some service tickets to quick remote resolutions, reducing cost and resolution time — especially for software and configuration issues.
